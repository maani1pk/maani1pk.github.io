The Last Star: Into the Dark Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 (for version 1.0 and later)

Developer (data controller for the app): Abdur Rehman Arshad, trading as Abdur Studio, Lahore, Pakistan

App: The Last Star: Into the Dark, for iPhone and iPad (com.abdurstudio.thelaststar)

Contact: maani1pk@gmail.com

This policy explains what information The Last Star: Into the Dark handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. The game is a single-player puzzle platformer. It has no accounts, no login, no chat, no user-generated content and no servers of our own. It is free to download, shows advertising, and has optional in-app purchases. The advertising involves the collection of some data from your device, described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Last Star saves your progress in a file in the app's private storage on your device. This file contains:

  • the chapter and checkpoint you have reached, and the stars you have found in each chapter;
  • the stars you can spend on opening chapters, and which chapters you have opened;
  • which purchases you own (a yes or no for each product, not any payment detail);
  • your nightly gift progress and the date you last took it, and counters used to space ads out (for example the number of reward ads watched today);
  • your settings, such as sound and music.

This file is not sent to us or to anyone else by the game. We have no copy of it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud device backup) may include app data according to your device settings; that is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and does not ask for those permissions. The game plays offline; it uses the internet only for ads and for the App Store.

2. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (opening the last two acts, the Full Journey, removing between-chapter ads, and a pouch of stars) are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, card or other payment details. The game uses Unity's in-app purchasing library to talk to the App Store, and only records on your device which products you own. Restore purchases in Settings asks the App Store which purchases your Apple ID owns. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK): an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a chapter's end screen (never after chapters 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or 30, never twice within a few minutes, and never after you have bought anything), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for bonus stars (at most five a day). There are no banner ads, no ads while you are playing a chapter, and no ads when the app opens. The ad purchase Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads between chapters; the optional reward ads remain, because you choose them.

To request, show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects data from your device and shares it with Google. According to Google's own disclosures, this includes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device;
  • Device and advertising identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking, see section 6) and other app- or developer-scoped identifiers;
  • Product interaction data: for example app launches, taps and ad/video views;
  • Advertising data: for example which ads you have seen;
  • Diagnostic and performance information: for example app launch time, hang rate, energy usage and crash logs relating to the SDK.

Google uses this data for advertising (including, where you have allowed it, personalized ads), ad measurement and analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. We (the developer) do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we see only aggregated reports (for example the number of ads shown) in our AdMob account.

Google's processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data for advertising

Source of the list above: Google's SDK data disclosure for iOS.

4. The game engine

The game is made with the Unity engine. We have switched off Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and Unity crash reporting in this game, so it sends no gameplay or usage information to Unity through them. Unity's purchasing library, used only for the App Store purchases in section 2, starts a basic Unity service that it needs to work; Unity's own notice is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland) and US state privacy choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before any ads are requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google consent management tool). You can choose whether your data may be used for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device. If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which still require some data processing (for example for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement).

Change your choice at any time: open Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices in the game. This button appears wherever a privacy choice applies.

Depending on where you live in the United States, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or of targeted advertising. Where Google's tools support it in your state, a privacy message or the Ad privacy choices entry lets you opt out. You can also contact us at the address below.

6. Your device controls

  • iOS / iPadOS: the game can access the advertising identifier only if you allow it in the "Allow tracking" prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. If tracking is not allowed, ads are still shown but the advertising identifier is not sent.
  • You can manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Uninstalling the game stops all collection by the app and the ad SDK.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it, except that the ad SDK sends the data described in section 3 directly to Google and, through its ad systems, to the advertisers and ad technology partners Google works with, as described in Google's policies. Some US state laws may treat targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; see section 5 for your choices. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Retention

  • Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google's retention policies. We do not hold copies.
  • If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

9. Children

The Last Star is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some scenes show creatures chasing the player, in silhouette. If you believe a child has used the game and that data has been collected through ads, contact us at maani1pk@gmail.com and we will help, including by explaining how to reset the device advertising identifier or delete app data.

10. Your rights and legal bases

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information, without being discriminated against for using these rights.

Because the game has no accounts and we do not receive identifiable data, we usually cannot link any data to you. In practice:

  • your game data can be deleted by you directly (uninstall the app);
  • ad consent can be withdrawn in Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices;
  • requests about data held by Google can be made through Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy;
  • you can contact us at maani1pk@gmail.com with any request and we will respond within the time the law requires.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Where GDPR applies, the legal basis for ad processing is your consent (personalized ads, and storing or reading information such as the advertising identifier on your device) and our legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads and preventing fraud (non-personalized or limited ads), as permitted by law.

11. International transfers

We are based in Pakistan, and the ad service is run by a company in another country. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be processed by Google in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your personal data ourselves, other than emails you send us.

12. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the game keeps the data it handles to a minimum.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example if we add new features or services that handle data), we will update this page and the effective date above, and, where required, ask for your consent again.

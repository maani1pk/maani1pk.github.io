Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Hold the ring on the left to move; push it down to crouch. Jump is on the right. When something can be grabbed (a crate, a lever) a Grab button appears; hold it to push or pull. When Lumi can do something (light a candle, wake a vine, push back moss) a Lumi button appears. The two bars at the top open the pause menu.

How do I get past the creatures?

Each one hunts differently. Hounds follow your trail but lose it on ladders and in water. Listeners come to noise: crouch to walk silently. Wardens watch an area: wait in cover until they turn away. Hiding places such as sacks and lockers let you stay out of sight. If you are caught, you restart at the last checkpoint.

How do I open the next chapters?

Chapters 1 to 5 are open from the start. Chapters 6 to 20 each cost stars to open the first time you reach them (4, 6 and 8 stars in the three groups of five). You earn stars by finding the three stars hidden in each chapter, by taking tonight's gift in the main menu, or by watching an optional ad on the chapter screen. Chapters 21 to 30 (Acts V and VI) are an optional purchase, or part of the Full Journey.

What is the nightly gift?

Open Gift in the main menu once a night for stars: 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 and then 10 on the seventh night. If you miss a night, it starts again at night one. You can watch an ad to double a gift.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. The App Store tells the game what your Apple ID has bought. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device only; there is no account. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and it cannot be restored by us. Purchases can always be restored as above.

Ads and privacy choices

The Last Star shows ads from Google AdMob. There are no ads while you play a chapter. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the full-screen ads between chapters; optional reward ads stay because you choose them. Where a privacy choice applies, you can change it in Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices, and you can allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline; ads and purchases need an internet connection.

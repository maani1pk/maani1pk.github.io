A boy. A small light. A world that went dark. Thirty silhouette chapters of puzzles and stealth, for iPhone and iPad.

Follow the light

Leo and Lumi

A boy and his glowing companion cross a drowned, rain-soaked world at night, following the trace of one last star.

Lumi's light

Wake vines, light candles, push back creeping moss and keep shy creatures away.

Things that hunt

Hounds follow your trail, listeners come to every noise, wardens watch the dark. Hide, slip past or outrun them.

Hands-on puzzles

Push and pull crates, ride lifts, swing across gaps, wade through rising water.

Six acts, thirty chapters

  • The Drowned City
  • Under the New Star
  • The Lost Districts
  • The Night Market and Below
  • Beyond the Gate
  • The Last Star

Find the three stars hidden in every chapter. Chapters 1 to 5 are open from the start; the rest open with the stars you find, tonight's gift or an ad you choose to watch. The game contains ads and optional in-app purchases, and plays offline.

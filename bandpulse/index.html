Five ways to play

Pick an instrument, find your pulse

Every song can be played on every instrument, and each one plays differently.

Piano Rush

4 to 6 keys, chords, long notes and speed bursts.

Guitar Rush

Coloured frets, power chords, solos and star notes that charge Overdrive.

Drum Rush

Kick, snare, hi-hat and cymbal pads, with fills and crashes.

DJ Remix

Swipe to scratch, hold long lines and ride the build-up into the drop.

Music Fusion

One song, three instruments: switch between piano, guitar and drums as the sections change.

About the game

A whole band in your pocket

BandPulse is a single-player rhythm game made for quick sessions. Pick a song, pick an instrument and play: a run takes a couple of minutes, and there is always another star, another mission or another instrument to try.

The game plays offline, keeps your progress on your device and never asks you to sign up.

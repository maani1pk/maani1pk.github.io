Tennis Ball Master 3D has been on the App Store since October 2025. Version 2.0 is a full rebuild in Unity: tap to hit, swipe to aim, Soft, Normal and Power shots, and slow motion that helps you time every return.

2.0 adds Classic, Survival, Time Attack and Target Rush modes, a 30-level Career with season bosses, four bracket tournaments, 30 missions and a Customize screen for 27 players. It was submitted to the App Store in November 2026.

Highlights

  • Live on the App Store (universal iPhone and iPad)
  • Four modes: Classic, Survival, Time Attack, Target Rush
  • 30-level Career with 3-star ratings and boss matches
  • Four tournaments, 30 missions, 27 customizable players
  • Plays offline; AdMob ads with consent and App Tracking Transparency

Tennis Ball Master 3D has been on the App Store since October 2025 (version 1.0). Matches are played with simple drag-and-swipe shots, and every hit lets you shape spin, speed and placement.

A larger update is in development: new game modes, a career mode and missions.

Highlights

  • Live on the App Store (version 1.0, universal iPhone and iPad)
  • Grass, clay and hard court surfaces
  • Topspin, slice, drop shot and smash
  • Update with new modes and a career mode in development

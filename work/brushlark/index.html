Brushlark: AR Tracing Camera

Trace sketches onto real paper through your phone camera.

Brushlark puts a sketch over the live camera view so you can trace it onto paper. You can move, scale, rotate, lock and fade the sketch, then draw it by hand. Or draw directly on screen with pencil, pen, brush and eraser, with undo, redo and PNG export.

A Drawing School adds 40 guided lessons that build each picture in stages. The app has been installed and checked on a Galaxy S21; store release and iOS are still to come.

Highlights

  • 70 original sketches in 11 categories
  • 40 step-by-step lessons
  • Tracing camera with lock, opacity, size and rotate
  • Remove-ads purchase and consent flow built in

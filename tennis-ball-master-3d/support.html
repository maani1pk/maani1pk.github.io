Tennis Ball Master 3D Support

Need help with Tennis Ball Master 3D? Email maani1pk@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I hit the ball?

When the ball comes your way the game slows down and shows Swipe or tap to hit! Tap to send the ball straight back, or swipe sideways to aim it left or right. The Soft, Normal and Power buttons at the bottom choose the kind of shot.

How do I serve?

Touch the screen when the power bar is high, then swipe or tap. The higher the bar, the faster the serve.

How do I unlock players, courts and hats?

Four players are free; the others cost gems. The Steel Night court costs coins, and each hat style is bought once with coins and then works for every player. Hair and outfit colors are free. Coins and gems come from matches, Career levels, missions, tournaments and the daily gift. There are no in-app purchases.

How does the Career work?

The Career has 30 levels in three seasons. Win a level to open the next one, and earn up to 3 stars by losing as few points as you can. Every tenth level is a season boss on the Steel Night court.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device only; there is no account. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes progress, and it cannot be restored by us.

Ads and privacy choices

Tennis Ball Master 3D is free and shows ads: from Google AdMob in the App Store version, and from HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) in the HUAWEI AppGallery version. There are no ads during a match. You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy choices where a choice applies, and on iPhone you can allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline; ads need an internet connection.

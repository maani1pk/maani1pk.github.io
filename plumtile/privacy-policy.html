Plumtile Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Developer: Abdur Studio

App: Plumtile: Block Puzzle Game, iPhone and iPad (com.abdurstudio.plumtile)

Contact: maani1pk@gmail.com

Note: Drafted for the owner's review; it is not legal advice.

This policy explains what information Plumtile handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. Plumtile is an offline, single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no social features, no user-generated content and no servers of our own. It is free to play and shows advertising, and that advertising involves the collection of some data from your device by Google, described below.

1. Who we are

Plumtile is published by Abdur Studio ("we", "us"), an independent game developer. You can reach us at maani1pk@gmail.com. The game sends no data to us. We do not hold personal data about players, other than emails you choose to send us.

2. What the game stores on your device

Plumtile saves the following on your device only:

  • your progress (Adventure levels and chapters, puzzles solved, Classic and Blitz best scores, Daily Challenge progress and tutorial status);
  • your coins and what you have unlocked (chapters, puzzle sets, themes);
  • your settings and your purchase status (for example whether Full Game or Remove Ads is active), and a count of finished games used to space out full-screen ads;
  • your ad consent choice, which is stored by Google's consent tool on your device.

This data is not sent to us by the game, and we have no copy of it. There is no cloud save. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud backup) may include app data according to your device settings; that is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

3. Ads and consent

Plumtile is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK):

  • Full-screen ads appear only between games, never during play, and at most every third finished game. They are removed by the Full Game or Remove Ads purchase.
  • Rewarded videos are optional and you choose to watch them (for free rescue blocks, to double a level reward, or for 50 free coins, up to 5 a day).
  • There are no banner ads.

To request, show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data from your device and shares it with Google. According to Google's published disclosure, this can include:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier or other app- or developer-scoped identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • advertising data, such as which ads you have seen, and product interaction data such as taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs, app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Google states that it uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's page App Store data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK for the current list. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we see only aggregated reports (for example the number of ads shown) in our ad account.

Consent choices

Where required (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time by opening Settings › Privacy choices in the game. If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which can still involve some data processing (for example frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention).

Tracking on iPhone and iPad

On iOS the game asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it. If you say no, ads are still shown, but they may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You can also manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

4. In-app purchases

Plumtile offers optional purchases: coin packs (500, 1,600 and 4,500 coins), Full Game (lifetime; unlocks every chapter, puzzle set and theme and removes ads) and Remove Ads (lifetime; removes the full-screen ads only). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The game receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded and never sees your payment details. To talk to the App Store the game uses the Unity In-App Purchasing package from Unity Technologies (see section 5). Purchases can be restored from Settings using the same Apple ID.

5. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Google LLC): ads and the consent form, as described in section 3. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Apple: the App Store, payments for purchases and the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies): a software package that connects the game to the App Store for purchases. This game uses a version of the package that, according to Unity's documentation, collects default data such as a player identifier, an installation identifier, device information, session identifiers and the country of the device, and uses it to operate and improve the purchasing service. This data goes to Unity, not to us. See Unity's IAP privacy and consent documentation and the Unity Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. The ad software described above sends data directly to Google, and, through Google's ad systems, to advertisers and ad technology partners as described in Google's policies. Some US state laws may treat targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; see section 3 for your choices. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Children

Plumtile is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data has been collected through ads, contact us at maani1pk@gmail.com and we will help, including by explaining how to reset the device advertising identifier or delete the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Delete the app to remove all local data. There is nothing on a server to delete.
  • Advertising data is held by Google under Google's retention policies. We hold no copy.
  • If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, without being treated unfairly for using these rights.

The developer holds no personal data about players, because the game has no accounts and sends us nothing. In practice:

  • you can delete your game data yourself by deleting the app;
  • you can withdraw or change ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices, and tracking in iOS Settings;
  • Google handles advertising data under its own policy; requests about that data are made through Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy;
  • you can email maani1pk@gmail.com with any request and we will respond within the time the law requires.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above and, where required, ask for your consent again.

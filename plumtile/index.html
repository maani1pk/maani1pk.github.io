Questions

Is Plumtile free?

Yes, it is free to play. It shows ads (see the next question) and offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs of 500, 1,600 or 4,500 coins, Full Game (a one-time purchase that unlocks every chapter, puzzle set and theme and removes ads) and Remove Ads (a one-time purchase that removes the full-screen ads only).

How do the ads work?

Full-screen ads appear only between games, never during play, and at most every third finished game. There are no banner ads. You can also choose to watch optional rewarded videos for free rescue blocks, to double a level reward, or for 50 free coins (up to 5 times a day). Ads come from Google AdMob.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Plumtile is a single-player game that plays offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress. See Support.

What do you do with my data?

The game sends nothing to the developer. Google's ad software collects some data to show ads, and you can manage your choices in the game and in iOS settings. Read the full privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 13 or later.

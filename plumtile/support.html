Plumtile Support

Need help with Plumtile? Email maani1pk@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Full Game and Remove Ads are lifetime purchases tied to your Apple ID, so they can be restored on any iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple ID. Coin packs are consumable: they are spent as you play and cannot be restored. If a purchase does not appear, check your connection and your Apple ID, then email us.

How do I remove ads?

Plumtile is free and shows ads. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes the full-screen ads only. Full Game is a one-time purchase that unlocks every chapter, puzzle set and theme and also removes ads. Optional rewarded videos, which you choose to watch for rewards, remain available. There are no banner ads, and full-screen ads never appear during play.

Can I replay the tutorial?

The tutorial runs on your first launch. To see it again, look for the tutorial option in Settings. If it is not listed in your version, delete and reinstall the app, but note that this also deletes your progress (see below). Email us if you cannot find it.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Progress, coins and settings are saved on your device only; there is no account and no cloud save. Deleting the app deletes them, and we cannot restore them because we do not have a copy. Purchases of Full Game and Remove Ads can be restored (see above); coins and level progress cannot.

How do I change my privacy and ad choices?

Where a consent choice applies (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows Google's consent form. You can reopen it at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad you can also allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Saying no only means less relevant ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Plumtile is a free game for iPhone and iPad and needs iOS 13 or later.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline. Ads and purchases need an internet connection.

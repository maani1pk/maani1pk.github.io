Information for parents

We made this app the way we would want a child's app to be made: calm, honest and easy to control.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising of any kind and no analytics or tracking tools. It has no accounts, no chat and no links that take a child out of the app. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

The Parent Zone

The Parent Zone is behind a grown-up check: the screen shows three digits written as words and asks for them to be typed in. After three wrong tries it pauses for 30 seconds. Inside you will find a weekly play-time chart, a daily time limit (15, 30 or 60 minutes, or off), settings, the store and Restore Purchases. Leaving the zone locks it again.

Purchases

  • The app is free to download. Starter content is free forever.
  • There are two optional purchases: Unlock Everything (one time) and a Coin Pack (500 coins). There are no subscriptions.
  • Prices are set and shown by Apple App Store or Google Play in your currency. The app never sees card details.
  • Purchases can only be started inside the Parent Zone.
  • Restore Purchases (in the Parent Zone store) brings back what the same store account bought, for example after reinstalling or on a new device.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. See Support.

Coins, stars and stickers

Children earn stars for finishing activities and coins for finishing lessons. A child can spend 100 coins to open one locked tracing lesson. Coins can also be added by a grown-up with a Coin Pack in the Parent Zone. They are never random, never a prize box and never shown next to a price. Stars and stickers are just for fun.

Screen time and sound

Sessions are short by design. The daily limit shows a gentle rest screen when time is up. Sound, music, voice and vibration can each be switched off, and calm mode softens animation.

Languages

English, Spanish and French are available now.

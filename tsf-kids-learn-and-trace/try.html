Try tracing the letter A

This is how children learn in the app. Start at the green dot and drag along the dotted path with your mouse, finger or pen. Three strokes make an A.

The demo runs in your browser only. Nothing you draw is saved or sent anywhere. In the app there are 26 letters, numbers, shapes and animals to trace.

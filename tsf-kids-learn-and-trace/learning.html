Letters

Each letter shows numbered start dots, direction arrows and a soft guide road. Children trace with a finger; staying on the road earns up to three stars. The letter's sound can be played at any time.

Numbers

Numbers 0 to 20 are traced the same way. For 1 to 10 a row of small stars appears so a child can count the quantity while writing the numeral.

Shapes and animals

Shapes build early hand control with curves and corners. Animals are simple outlines a child can finish in a minute, then color.

Mini-games

The games are grouped in six worlds: alphabet, numbers, colors, shapes, animals and play. They include matching, sorting, finding, memory cards, simple jigsaws and mixed challenges. A daily challenge offers a small bonus once a day.

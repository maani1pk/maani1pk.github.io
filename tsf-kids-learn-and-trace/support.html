Support

Email us any time at maani1pk@gmail.com. Please tell us your device model, Android or iOS version and what happened.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Parent Zone (the gear on the home screen, then type the three numbers shown as words), open the store and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase and make sure you are online.

How do I buy Unlock Everything or coins?

Purchases are only available inside the Parent Zone. When a child taps a locked lesson, the app offers to spend coins or to ask a grown-up, who can open the Parent Zone and choose Unlock Everything or a Coin Pack. There are no subscriptions.

I forgot the parent code.

The code is different every time: the screen shows three digits written as words (for example "four · one · nine") and you type them as numbers. After three wrong tries it waits 30 seconds.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On Google Play, use your order history on play.google.com or the Play Store app. On iOS, use reportaproblem.apple.com. We are happy to help you find the right place.

Does the app need internet?

No. Learning, drawing and games work offline. Only buying and restoring need a connection to the store.

My purchase does not show up.

Check you are signed in with the account that bought it, go online and tap Restore purchases in the Parent Zone store. If a payment is "pending" (for example waiting for family approval) the content unlocks as soon as the store confirms it.

How do I delete my child's data?

All data is stored on the device. In the Parent Zone open Settings and choose "Delete all saved art" or "Reset all progress". Uninstalling the app removes everything. We hold no copy.

Does the app show ads or collect data?

No ads and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Which languages and devices are supported?

English, Spanish and French on Android phones and tablets, and iPhone and iPad.

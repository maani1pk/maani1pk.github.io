Parent promise

Built to be trusted with a child.

  • No advertising of any kind. No banners, no video ads, no ad SDKs inside the app.
  • No tracking and no accounts. Progress and artwork stay on the device.
  • Parent Zone. Purchases and settings sit behind a grown-up check.
  • One-time purchases. No subscriptions, no trials that turn into charges.
  • Restore Purchases brings your purchases back on a new device.

Read the parent information or the privacy policy.

What is free, what is optional

  • Free: letters A to P, numbers 0 to 15, five shapes, six animals, seven coloring pages, drawing and all mini-games.
  • Unlock Everything (one payment): letters Q to Z, numbers 16 to 20, three more shapes, Lion, Elephant and two more coloring pages.
  • Coin Pack (optional): 500 coins. Each locked lesson costs 100 coins.

Children also earn coins by finishing lessons. Coins only open tracing lessons: there are no random prizes, and no price is ever shown to a child.

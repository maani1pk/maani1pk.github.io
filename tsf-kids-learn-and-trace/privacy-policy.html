Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Developer: Abdur Studio
App: TSF Kids Learn and Trace, iPhone, iPad and Android (com.abdurstudio.tsfkidslearntrace)
Contact: maani1pk@gmail.com

Note: drafted for the owner's review; it is not legal advice.

This policy explains what information TSF Kids Learn and Trace handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. The app is an offline learning app for young children. It has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no social features and no servers of our own. The app sends no data to us.

1. Who we are

TSF Kids Learn and Trace is published by Abdur Studio ("we", "us"), an independent app developer. You can reach us at maani1pk@gmail.com. We do not hold personal data about children or parents who use the app, other than emails you choose to send us.

2. What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following on the device only:

  • progress (stars, coins, stickers, badges and finished levels);
  • settings (sound, language, theme and time limit);
  • the child's first name or nickname, only if a grown-up chooses to type one in Settings;
  • saved drawings and coloring pages;
  • which purchases the store account owns, and a record of coin packs already credited so they are never credited twice.

This data stays in the app's private storage. We do not receive it and have no copy. There is no cloud save. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Android Backup) may include app data according to your device settings; that is controlled by you and Apple or Google, not by us.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, device identifiers for tracking, or behavioural data. The app contains no advertising or analytics software and no crash-reporting, social or attribution services, and we do not use third-party tracking.

4. In-app purchases

The app is free to download. A grown-up can buy two optional items inside the Parent Zone: Unlock Everything (a one-time purchase) and a Coin Pack (500 coins that a child spends to open lessons). Purchases and the coin counter are behind a grown-up check.

Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store payment card details, and we do not receive your name or email from the store. The app asks the store which purchases the account owns so it can unlock them and restore them after reinstalling. This uses the platform billing service of your device and the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which talks only to the platform store. Please read Apple's or Google's privacy policy for how they handle purchase data.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: purchases, restore and app delivery.
  • Device text-to-speech and sound: spoken prompts use the device's own voice engine when available. Depending on the device, that engine may use a network voice; we do not control it.

6. Children

The app is made for children, including children under 13, and is designed for the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up check (the "Parent Zone"). The check helps keep young children out of those areas; it is not a way of verifying identity or giving consent. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not ask for verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We design the app to follow the principles of children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on the device can be removed at any time: Settings has "Delete all saved art" and "Reset all progress", and uninstalling the app removes everything. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

8. This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, social widgets or third-party fonts. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about users of the app, there is normally nothing to provide, but you can write to maani1pk@gmail.com and we will answer within a reasonable time, typically within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example if a feature that collects data were ever added), we will update this policy and the app's store listings before release, and we will not collect personal information from children without the consent the law requires. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

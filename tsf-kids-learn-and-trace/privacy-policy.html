Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. Applies to the TSF Kids Learn and Trace app for Android and iOS ("the app") and to this website. The app is published by Abdur Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: maani1pk@gmail.com.

In short

  • The app is designed for children and contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It has no accounts. We do not ask a child for a name, email, location, photo, voice recording or any contact detail.
  • Progress, settings and artwork are stored on the device only. We do not receive them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from children or from parents through the app, and the app does not send progress or usage data to our servers. We do not operate a server for the app.

Stored on your device

  • Progress (stars, coins, stickers, badges, finished levels), settings (sound, language, theme, time limit), the child's first name or nickname if a grown-up chooses to type one in Settings, and saved drawings and coloring pages.
  • This data stays in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes it. A grown-up can also delete saved art or reset progress in Settings.
  • Your device's own backup service (for example Android Backup or iCloud) may copy app data according to your device settings. We do not control that.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, device identifiers for tracking, or behavioural data. The app does not contain advertising or analytics software, and we do not use third-party tracking.

Purchases and billing

Extra content can be bought by a grown-up inside the Parent Zone. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see or store payment card details, and we do not receive your name or email from the store. The app asks the store which purchases the store account owns so that it can unlock them and restore them after reinstalling. This uses the platform billing service of your device and, on Android and iOS, the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which talks only to the platform store. Please read the privacy policy of Google or Apple for how they handle purchase data.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: purchases, restore and app delivery.
  • Device text-to-speech and sound: spoken prompts use the device's own voice engine when available.
  • The app does not include advertising networks, analytics, crash-reporting, social or attribution services.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up check (the "Parent Zone"). The check helps keep young children out of those areas; it is not a method of verifying a person's identity or giving consent. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not ask for verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We design the app to follow the principles of children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families requirements.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on the device can be removed at any time: Settings has "Delete all saved art" and "Reset all progress", and uninstalling the app removes everything. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, social widgets or third-party fonts. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Security

Because the app stores data only on the device and has no accounts, the main protection is your device's own security. Purchases are authorised by the store.

Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about users of the app, there is normally nothing to provide, but you can write to maani1pk@gmail.com and we will answer within a reasonable time, typically within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example if a feature that collects data were ever added), we will update this policy and the app's store listings before release, and we will not collect personal information from children without the consent the law requires.

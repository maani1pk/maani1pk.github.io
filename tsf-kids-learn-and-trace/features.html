Features made for small hands

Every screen is designed for short, happy sessions that a child can run alone and a parent can trust.

Guided stroke tracing

A glowing road, numbered start dots and arrows show the stroke order. A finger demonstration plays the first time, and again after a few missed tries. Gentle misses never punish: the child simply tries again.

A friendly guide

Pip the guide cheers, points and encourages. Sounds and a spoken voice can be switched on or off by a grown-up.

Stars, stickers and badges

Every finished trace earns stars. Stars unlock 15 collectible stickers and 11 badges, so progress is visible without pressure.

Coins for learning

Finishing lessons earns coins. Coins can open a single locked tracing item. They are earned only, never sold, and never random: no loot boxes, no gambling mechanics.

Coloring and drawing

Fill coloring pages with a bright palette, undo, clear and save. Draw freely and keep artwork in the on-device gallery.

41 mini-games, 870 levels

Short games for letters, numbers, colors, shapes and animals. Levels open one after another and every level can be completed.

Calm mode and gentle sound

A calm setting softens animation and fades. Volume, music, voice and vibration each have their own switch.

Three languages

English, Spanish and French. More languages are shown as coming soon and are not offered until they are complete.

Works offline

Learning does not need internet. Only buying or restoring a purchase needs a connection to the app store.

TSF Kids Learn and Trace

A calm, ad-free tracing and learning app for young children. Free to download, with two optional purchases that only a grown-up can make.

About me

A developer who ships to the device, not just the editor.

I build in Unity and C#: mobile games, kids' apps, AR experiences on phones and VR experiences on Meta Quest. I work through the whole chain — gameplay and UI, builds for Android and iOS, testing on real hardware, and tracking down the crashes and frame drops that only show up there.

My current work spans AR Foundation and ARCore face tracking, OpenXR with the XR Interaction Toolkit on Quest 3, and app-store preparation including ad integration for child-directed apps. I also lead Vimersive Studio as CEO.

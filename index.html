Abdur Studio

My own label for games, mobile apps, AR/VR and interactive experiences. Small by design: you work directly with the developer building your product, from prototype through release.

I build in Unity and C#, and I work through the whole chain: gameplay and UI, builds for Android and iOS, testing on real phones and headsets, and tracking down the crashes and frame drops that only show up there.
